ENGINEERING & HARDWARE

Dedicated Materials for Kitchens & Rooms

Every Nalam piece is built with purpose-selected boards—heavy-duty moisture-ready construction for kitchens and warp-resistant precision blockboard for bedrooms and living units.

For Kitchens

Good Wood / Top Wood

Dedicated for kitchens: melamine-faced sandwich blockboard carcasses delivering superior screw-holding strength and durability.

For Rooms & Units

16mm Warp-Resistant Blockboard

Dedicated for rooms (bedrooms, dressings, vanities & cabinets): 16mm melamine-faced blockboard precision-joined with minimal gaps.

Sink & WC Units

100% Waterproof Boardium

High-density 100% water-resistant Boardium engineered for kitchen sink bases and all WC vanity units to prevent moisture swelling.

Motion Hardware

Turkish SAMET Hinges

Original Turkish SAMET soft-close hydraulic hinges engineered for silent, effortless door closing across tens of thousands of cycles.

Motion Hardware

Italian FGV Runners

Concealed Italian FGV undermount soft-close drawer runners delivering smooth full-extension glide under heavy daily loads.
