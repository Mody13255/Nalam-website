ENGINEERING & HARDWARE

Built for Decades of Daily Use

Luxury aesthetics mean nothing without structural integrity underneath. Every Nalam unit is engineered with certified boards and European/Turkish motion hardware.

01

Good Wood / Top Wood

Premium melamine-faced sandwich blockboard carcasses providing superior screw-holding strength, zero warping, and clean interior finishes.

02

100% Waterproof Boardium

Dedicated high-density, 100% water-resistant Boardium used for kitchen sink bases and all WC vanity units to eliminate moisture swelling forever.

03

Turkish SAMET Hinges

Original Turkish SAMET soft-close hydraulic hinges engineered for silent, effortless door closing across tens of thousands of cycles.

04

Italian FGV Runners

Concealed Italian FGV undermount soft-close drawer runners delivering smooth full-extension glide even under heavy cookware loads.
